A card on the board is a live task from your tracker: its type, key, title and status. Statuses refresh every 30 seconds with one batched request per board, and closed tasks are struck through.

## Add cards by key, link or query

Pick the card tool (`C`) and type one of these:

- a key, such as `PROJ-123`;
- a link to the task;
- a range of keys;
- a query, like JQL in Jira.

Several new cards land close together in columns of five, read top to bottom. What each tracker takes, with examples, is in [What you can add](../what-you-can-add/).

## Paste tasks

Press `⌘V` (`Ctrl+V`) on the board. Links copied from your tracker tabs or from GitHub, keys and ranges of keys become cards. Any other text becomes a sticky note, placed at the cursor.

## Read a card

- **Details:** click a card for assignee, priority, last update and a link to the task.
- **Links** open in a new tab. **Settings → Preferences** can open them in this one.
- **Collapse** cards to one line when the board gets busy: select them and use the bar above the selection.

## Check that your tracker syncs

A colored dot in the corner of the screen shows whether every tracker syncs. Click it to see why one fails.
