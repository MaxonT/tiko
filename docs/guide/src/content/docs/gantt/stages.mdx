:::note[Needs Planning]
Planning is on in a new tiko, and always on on the [public demo](https://demo.tiko.run). An instance set up before keeps the switch where it was; an admin turns it on in **Settings → Stages and people**. Switching it off keeps every stage: the Gantt then shows one bar per task again.
:::

## Stages

With Planning on, a row is cut into stages: discovery, design, build, a pause, or whatever your team calls its phases. A task card dropped into the Gantt becomes a row with one bar, as before.

- **Add a stage:** drag across free days of a row. A click adds one week. A new stage takes the type that follows the previous one.
- **Pick its type or delete it:** click the stage. A bar that has no stages yet reads "No stage" until you pick one.
- **Move or stretch it:** drag a stage to move it, or its ends to change its dates. On its own a stage stops at its neighbours, so stages never overlap.
- **Shift the rest of the plan:** hold `Alt` while moving or stretching a stage to the right, and it pushes the later stages of its row by the same days, gaps kept, so a plan shifts by a week in one drag.
- **Mark a stage done:** click it and tick **Done**. A done stage is hatched and no longer counts in anyone's workload or warns about their time off. A row with every stage done is [done](../#track-progress); its own box ticks all its stages at once. A tracker task's stages are ticked the same way, but the task itself is done only when your tracker says so.

### Stage types

Stage types belong to the whole tiko. Add one from a stage with **New type**; rename, recolor or archive it in **Settings → Stages and people**. An archived type stays on the stages that use it, and new stages cannot pick it.

## People on stages

Each stage can name the people planned for it. They come from one list for the whole tiko, in **Settings → Stages and people**, also under **⋯ → People** in the Gantt controls. Most of them never sign in, so this list is separate from the accounts in **Settings → People**.

- **Pick someone:** type a name in a stage's popover to pick someone from the list.
- **Add someone new:** a name nobody has yet offers **Add "Name" to people**.
- **From tracker:** assignees of the tasks in this Gantt who are not in the list show as **From tracker**. Picking one adds them; nobody is imported until you pick them.

The tracker's assignee and the stage's people are independent: the tracker says who owns the task now, a stage says who is planned for that phase.

- **Where names show:** after the stage type, or centered under each stage with **⋯ → Under each stage**. When they do not fit, the stage shows a people icon with a count; click the stage for the list.
- **Archived people** stay on old stages, muted, and are not offered again.
- **Public links:** someone viewing a public link sees the names but cannot change them.

On the [public demo](https://demo.tiko.run) the people and stage types are made up and yours to change: every visitor gets a copy of their own.
