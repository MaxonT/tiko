Around the cards from your tracker, a board holds frames, notes, shapes, text, code, arrows, pictures and stickers. The board is saved on the server and reopens exactly as you left it.

## Add elements

| Element | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | `F` | Groups cards. Drag cards in and out; moving a frame moves everything inside. Takes a fill color. |
| **Sticky note** | `N` | Holds up to 2000 characters; the text shrinks to fit the note. |
| **Shape** | `S` | Rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, rhombus, triangle, parallelogram, hexagon or cylinder, for a flowchart next to the tasks. Holds a short text and takes a fill color. |
| **Text** | `T` | Text anywhere on the board. |
| **Code** | `K` | A piece of code with its colors, see [Code](#code). |
| **Arrow** | | Joins any two elements, or points at an empty spot. |
| **Picture** | `I` | A screenshot or a photo, see [Pictures](#pictures). |
| **Sticker** | `E` | An animated emoji or your team's own, see [Stickers](#stickers). |

Pick a shape, then drag to set its size or click to place it. Each creation tool shows its icon next to the crosshair, so you see which one is picked.

## Code

A code block holds up to 10,000 characters of code in a monospaced font, colored in the language you pick.

- **Title:** select the block and click **Add a title** in its header. Search finds a block by its title and by its code.
- **Language:** select the block and pick it in its header, or type a few letters to find it (`py`, `ts`, `yml` work too): Bash, C, C#, C++, CSS, Dockerfile, Go, GraphQL, HCL, HTML, Java, JavaScript, JSON, Kotlin, Markdown, PHP, PowerShell, Python, Ruby, Rust, SQL, Swift, TOML, TSX, TypeScript, XML, YAML or plain text. The colors follow the light or dark theme.
- **Edit:** double-click the code. `Tab` indents the line or the selected lines by two spaces, `Shift+Tab` moves them back, `Esc` finishes.
- **Copy** in the header puts the code on your clipboard as plain text, on a board you can only view too.
- **Fold** shows only the header with the title, or the first line when there is none, and the number of lines, for everyone. On a board you can only view, unfolding opens the block for you alone.
- **Turn into code:** select a text or a sticky and pick **Turn into code** in the bar above it; a code block turns back with **Turn into text**. Arrows and reminders stay on it. A sticky loses its color; a title becomes the first line of the text.

Long lines wrap; drag a side of the block to make it wider.

## Pictures

Add a picture in any of three ways:

- paste a screenshot or a copied picture with `⌘V` (`Ctrl+V`);
- drop files from your computer onto the board;
- press `I`, or right-click the board and choose **Add image**, to pick files.

PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF up to 25 MB each. Location and camera data are removed from photos. See [Pictures](../data-and-upgrades/#pictures) for where they are kept.

- **Upload:** a picture shows the moment you add it and uploads behind it. If an upload fails, the picture says why and offers **Retry**.
- **An upload that never finishes**, for example because the tab was closed, says so after two minutes. Anyone who can edit the board can then pick the file again.
- **Speed:** the board loads a smaller copy that is as sharp as the picture is on your screen, and the original only when you zoom in close. A board full of 4K screenshots stays as quick to pan and zoom as one of notes.
- **GIFs** play when at least 80 pixels wide on your screen. Smaller, or when your system asks for reduced motion, a GIF shows its first frame.

## Stickers

Press `E` or the sticker button in the toolbar. Click a sticker to put it in the middle of the view, or drag it where it goes.

- **The panel** holds about 880 animated emoji in sections, a **Recent** tab and a search by name. A sticker plays when the pointer rests on it; on the board it plays like a GIF.
- **Your team's stickers** live in the **Workspace** tab. Select a picture on the board and pick **Save to stickers** in the bar above it, and everyone in the workspace finds it there.
- **Removing** a sticker from the panel leaves it on the boards that show it.

The built-in set is [Noto Animated Emoji](https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/) by Google, under CC BY 4.0.

## Select and change elements

Drag a box from an empty spot to select several elements. The box takes every element it touches, and a frame only when it covers the whole frame, so you can pick notes inside a frame without the frame.

A bar appears next to the selection: above it, or below it when there is no room. The bar shows what every selected element has in common:

- **Fill:** pick a color and every selected element takes it, with one Undo to bring them back. Rest the pointer on a color to see it on the board before you pick.
- **Opacity:** a slider fades the elements.
- **Outline:** a thicker or dashed outline for shapes.
- **Text:** size and bold.
- **Align:** with two or more elements, line up their edges or centers; with three or more, space them evenly. One Undo step.
- **Collapse** cards, add a **reminder**, **duplicate** and **delete**.

Each control shows only where every selected element can take it. A selection of several kinds starts with "N objects": open it and choose a kind to keep only those selected.

## Move, resize and rotate

- **Resize** from a corner, or from one side to change only the width or the height.
- **Smart guides** snap what you drag or resize to the edges, centers and gaps of its neighbors. Hold `⌘` (`Ctrl`) while you drag to move without them.
- **Copy by dragging:** hold `Alt` and start dragging; you can let go of `Alt` after that. The originals stay where they were, and their copies land where you drop them, arrows between them included. One Undo takes the copies back.
- **Rotate** a sticky note, a shape, a text or a picture: select it and drag from just outside any corner, where the pointer turns into a circular arrow. Hold `Shift` to turn in 15° steps.

The angle shows next to the pointer while you rotate, positive counterclockwise and negative clockwise, as in geometry. It lights up and pulls in when the element is a few degrees from straight, diagonal or upside down, so a crooked element is easy to set right again. A turned element still resizes from its own sides and corners, and its arrows follow its turned sides.

## Get around the board

- **Mouse and trackpad:** a mouse wheel zooms around the pointer and a two-finger swipe on a trackpad pans, as in most whiteboards. A pinch and `Ctrl` + wheel zoom, `Shift` + wheel pans sideways, and dragging with the wheel held pans. If your mouse pans instead of zooming, set **Settings → Preferences → Mouse wheel** to **Always zoom**.
- **Minimap:** the whole board in the bottom right corner, with the part you see framed. Click a spot to go there or drag the frame. The map button under the zoom buttons hides it, and your browser remembers that. Phones and narrow windows don't show it.
- **Back:** after search or the reminder list moves the board, press `B` or the arrow under the zoom buttons to return.
- **Zoom in on one element:** double-click it.
- **Switch boards** from the menu or the palette.

At 20% zoom and below, elements turn into blocks in their colors, with grey bars where the text was, and keep their arrows. A big board stays smooth to pan and zoom. Elements still select and move there; to edit text or draw a new arrow, zoom in.
