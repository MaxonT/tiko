## 💎 Improvements

- **Gantt:** A new tiko starts with Planning on, so stages, people and workload are there from the first board. Running instances keep their setting.
- **Sharing:** Enter adds the person in Share when the search finds exactly one.
- **Trackers:** Enter adds the ticked tasks, or the only one found, in the CalDAV task picker.
- **Guide:** Shorter pages with tables and steps instead of long paragraphs, and a sidebar grouped by what you do. Old links still land on the right section.
- **Site:** A Features menu on tiko.run, and a page on planning a Gantt from your tracker tasks.

## 🐞 Fixes

- **Board:** Fixed cards that a teammate or an agent adds showing Loading on your open board until the next refresh.
- **Board:** Fixed arrows drawn by an agent or a script looping through the top of both elements.
- **Board:** Fixed Fit view and the first open of a board, which left out everything off screen.
- **Board:** Fixed a pasted or dropped picture blinking a moment after it appears.
- **Board:** Fixed the "In vote" label staying on notes after a vote was ended and deleted.
- **Settings:** Fixed Enter doing nothing in forms such as sign-in in browsers that hold it back, such as Aside. Sign-in also takes a name and password the browser filled in.
- **Settings:** Fixed the X that did not close Settings; only Esc did.
- **Settings:** Fixed the sections list cut off in a short window.
- **Guide:** Fixed note boxes that stayed dark in the light theme.
