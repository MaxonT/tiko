Reactions and votes let a team agree without a word. In a retro, reactions show which notes people care about, and a vote picks the few to talk about first.

## React to a note

Hover a sticky note or a card, press the smiley under it and pick 👍 ❤️ 🎉 😂 🤔 or 👎. A click on a reaction adds yours or takes it back. To react to many notes at once, select them and pick an emoji under the smiley in the selection bar.

- **Open by design:** hover a reaction to see who put it, and press "and N more" for the whole list. Someone who has left the workspace shows as "Former member".
- **Who reacts:** people who can edit the board. Viewers see reactions with names; guests of a public link see only the counts.
- **At the same moment:** two people reacting at once both count.
- **Undo:** `⌘Z` does not take a reaction back; its own button does. A copy of a note starts with none.
- **No notifications:** nobody is notified about a reaction.

## Run a vote

<Steps>

1. Select the notes and cards, or a frame to take the ones inside it.
2. Press the ballot box in the selection bar.
3. Choose how many votes each person gets, whether several of them can go on one note, a timer, and whether the vote is anonymous.

</Steps>

Everyone on the board gets an invitation to join, viewers too. Guests of a public link do not see the vote.

## Vote

- **Give a vote:** a plus on each note in the vote gives it one of your votes. You can take votes back and move them until the end. The rest of the board steps back while you vote.
- **Done** puts the panel away and keeps your votes.
- **Hidden until the end:** nobody sees anyone else's votes until the vote ends, in an anonymous vote and in an open one.
- **Notes are held:** while the vote runs nobody can change or delete its notes, or delete the frame they sit in. They can still be moved and take reactions.

## Run the vote to its end

The panel shows how many people are done. Whoever started the vote and the board's owner can see who is still voting, add a minute or end the vote for everyone.

## Read the results

At the end the results open for everyone: the notes with votes, most first. Click one to go to it. An open vote also shows who gave how many votes to each note.

Past votes stay with the board: open them from **Votes** in the top bar, next to **Share**.

:::note[What an anonymous vote keeps]
The server keeps who gave which votes, because it has to hold each person to their number of votes. For an anonymous vote it never shows that to anyone; an admin with access to the database could still read it.
:::
